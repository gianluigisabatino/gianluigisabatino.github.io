Experience and training

Sep 2024 – Jan 2026

Laboratory Technician

Institute of Applied Synthetic Chemistry, TU Wien — Macromolecular Chemistry

On the WWTF project “Exploring the Glycome: Identifying Glycosylation-Related Biomarkers for severity stratification in Chronic Fatigue Syndrome” (€99,946), he established a biomarker-discovery pipeline for ME/CFS on more than 150 blood and milk samples. MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry and HILIC/RP-HPLC with fluorescence detection identified glycosylation patterns as inflammatory indicators, after fluorescent glycan conjugation was optimized for reproducible O-glycosylation, N-glycosylation and sialic acid analysis. With the MedUni Wien immunology team, hypo-glycosylation biomarkers were correlated with pro-inflammatory immune activation in post-infectious chronic fatigue. He presented the poster “N-glycan profiles of cells cultured with diverse monosaccharide treatments” at the AustroCarb workshop in Vienna on 14 February 2025.

In a parallel five-month project he synthesized and characterized acrylate-based hydrogels for biomedical applications, including thermal- and photo-initiated free-radical polymerization, with NMR and refractive-index analysis. He also prepared laboratory-course materials and supported the public outreach event The Long Night of Research.
